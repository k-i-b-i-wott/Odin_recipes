Preparation of ugali

Ingredients

  1. Honey
  2. Water
  3. maize flour

Other requirements

  1. Cooking gas
  2. cooking stick
  3. cooking pan

Steps

  1. Put mugs of water into to your pan
  2. Place the pan on the cooking gas and allow the water to simmer
  3. Add three table spoons of honey and stir it gently
  4. Now put the maize flour into the boiling water gently and stir it using a cooking stick till all the water is absorbed
  5. Cover the pan and lower the gas flame for 5 minutes and the serve the ugali into your favourite dish
  6. The ugali is now ready to be served with any stew
