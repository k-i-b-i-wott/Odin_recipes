Ingredients of cheese sauce

  1. 2 1/2 table spoon all purpose flour
  2. 120 gm cheddar cheese
  3. 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  4. 3 cup milk
  5. 1teasspoon salt

Steps

  • Put a pan over a medium-low flame and add butter on it,let it melt. add all p[urpose flour to it and stir well untill smooth paste is formed. Cook till the mixture turns bubbly. Grate the cheddar cheese in a medium bowl and keep it aside
  • Put another pan on a medium flame and add the grated cheesein it. Stir for a while and gradually add milk into the cheese mixture.Stir once and cook untill the mixture turns smooth and thick. Now add black pepper and salt to it as per your taste. Mix well all the ingredients and cook again. Switch off the flame once you see that the cheeses has mekted properly
  • Cheese sauce is ready to served with chips or baked vegetables
